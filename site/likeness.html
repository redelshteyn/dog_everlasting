Photographs and film

Before you begin

  • Even, natural light. Near a window, without flash.
  • Step back and zoom in slightly rather than coming close. Avoid the 0.5× wide lens; it distorts the face.
  • Hold the camera at their eye level.
  • Return on more than one day. Light, coat and mood all change.

02

Measurements

How to measure

  • Use a soft tape, with your dog standing square and relaxed.
  • Take each measurement twice, and add a round on another day. We average every round for you.
  • Leave anything you cannot measure blank.
