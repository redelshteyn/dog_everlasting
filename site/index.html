The Commission

01

The Model

While they are with you, we create a precise 3D model of your dog. Together we agree on how they will rest, and how they will be presented.

02

Your Veterinarian

We introduce ourselves to your veterinarian and provide our case. When the time comes, they prepare your dog and send them to us. There is nothing for you to arrange.

03

The Atelier

Their form is sculpted from the model while their coat is preserved by hand. One artisan works on your dog alone, over six to ten months.

04

The Final Likeness

Dogs change with the years. Before anything is final, we show you how they have changed since the model was made, and decide together how they will be remembered.

05

Homecoming

Your dog is assembled, returned home by fine-art shipper, and placed where they belong, with a signed certificate and lifetime care.

If your dog has already died, we begin with your veterinarian and model them from your photographs.

My dog has just died. What should I do?

Please call us at any hour, or begin a commission online. Contact your veterinarian, who can hold them for you. We send our case to your veterinarian overnight, and guide you through everything else.

How does my dog reach you?

Through your veterinarian. We provide them with our case, and when the time comes they prepare your dog and send them to us overnight. For larger dogs, we arrange a courier.

How does the Register work?

You pay $35 a month, credited in full toward your dog’s commission. We create a 3D model of your dog now, agree the pose and presentation with you, and keep the model safely until it is needed. We also arrange everything with your veterinarian in advance.

What if my dog changes after the model is made?

They will, and we plan for it. Before anything is final, we show you how they have changed since the model was made, and decide together whether they are remembered as they were then, or as they were at the end.

What happens to my payments when the time comes?

Everything you have paid is credited toward the commission, and the remaining balance is due at commission. Billing ends when the commission begins.

Can I cancel the Register, or move it to another dog?

You may cancel at any time, and billing stops. Payments already made are non-refundable. Each registration is for one named dog and cannot be transferred.

How lifelike is the result?

Our artisans work from your dog’s 3D model and your photographs throughout, and you see private previews along the way. The aim is simple: that when you walk into the room, it is them.

Can I choose the pose?

Yes. Resting, seated, lying with the head raised, or standing.

How are they cared for at home?

Keep them away from direct sunlight and heat, and dust gently with the brush provided. Every commission includes lifetime conservation care.

Do you accept commissions outside the United States?

Yes. International shipping and return is arranged by your Client Advisor.
